Common Staten Island Questions

Renovation Questions from Staten Island Homeowners

Do you handle DOB permits for Staten Island renovations?
Yes. We manage all required NYC Department of Buildings permits — plumbing, electrical, and structural — for renovations throughout Staten Island. All work meets DOB and Staten Island-specific code requirements.
Do you work on single-family homes across Staten Island?
Absolutely. Single-family homes make up most of Staten Island's housing stock — colonials, ranches, capes, and waterfront properties. We renovate every type, including full-home remodels and additions.
How long does a typical Staten Island home renovation take?
Full bathroom renovations take 3–5 weeks; kitchens 5–8 weeks. Full-home remodels and additions vary based on scope — typically 3–6 months. We provide a detailed timeline at quote.
Do you work on basements and finished lower levels?
Yes — Staten Island homes typically have full basements, and we specialize in finishing them: home theaters, in-law suites, gyms, playrooms, and rental units with proper egress.
What Staten Island neighborhoods do you serve?
All of Staten Island — St. George, Tottenville, Great Kills, New Dorp, Todt Hill, Eltingville, Annadale, Huguenot, South Beach, Stapleton, and everywhere in between.
Can I see real Staten Island renovation projects?
Yes — view our portfolio of completed bathroom and kitchen renovations across NYC. We can also arrange in-person walkthroughs of completed Staten Island projects with permission from past clients.
