Frequently Asked Questions

Do you install kitchen cabinets in Brooklyn brownstones and walk-ups?+
Yes. Brownstones and pre-war walk-ups are a Brooklyn specialty for us — we scribe, shim and level around out-of-square walls and floors so cabinets sit flush and tight even in older buildings and narrow galley kitchens.
How much does kitchen cabinet installation cost in Brooklyn?+
Cost depends on the number of cabinets, whether they need assembly, and add-ons like countertops and hardware. We give you a clear, itemized estimate up front — no surprises. Call (332) 277-0990 for a free Brooklyn estimate.
Can you assemble the IKEA or flat-pack cabinets I already bought?+
Absolutely — IKEA and flat-pack kitchens are common across Brooklyn, and we assemble and install flat-pack, IKEA, RTA and pre-built custom cabinets, including soft-close hinges and drawer slides, all leveled and secured.
Do you handle co-op and condo building requirements in Brooklyn?+
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured and can provide a certificate of insurance for Brooklyn co-op and condo building management, and we schedule installs around building access rules.
Can you remove and replace my old Brooklyn kitchen cabinets?+
Yes — cabinet replacement is one of our most-requested Brooklyn services. We remove and haul away the old cabinets, then install your new flat-pack, RTA, custom or pre-built units level and clean.
Which Brooklyn neighborhoods do you serve?+
We install kitchen cabinets across all of Brooklyn — including Park Slope, Williamsburg, Brooklyn Heights, Bay Ridge, Bushwick, Cobble Hill, DUMBO, Greenpoint and Flatbush.
