Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a bathroom renovation take?+
A typical NYC bathroom renovation takes 2–4 weeks from demo to completion, and a full gut remodel 4–6 weeks. Your timeline depends on the scope, material delivery and building scheduling.
How much does it cost to renovate a bathroom in NYC?+
Bathroom renovation cost in NYC depends on the size of the bathroom, the materials you choose, and how much of the layout changes. We give you a clear, itemized estimate up front so there are no surprises. Call (332) 277-0990 for a free cost estimate.
Do you renovate commercial bathrooms for restaurants, retail & offices?+
Yes. Alongside homeowners, we renovate commercial bathrooms and restrooms for restaurants, retail stores and offices across NYC — with durable finishes and scheduling that keeps your business running.
Do you provide design services?+
Yes — we offer free design consultations. Our team helps you plan layouts, choose materials, fixtures and finishes, and visualize your space before any work begins.
Do you work with co-op and condo buildings?+
Yes — we regularly remodel bathrooms in NYC co-ops and condos. We work within building rules and alteration agreements, coordinate board approvals, and carry the insurance most buildings require.
How much does a bathroom remodel cost in NYC?+
A bathroom remodel in NYC is priced by size, fixtures and tile — from light refreshes to full gut remodels. As upfront bathroom remodel contractors, we give you a free, itemized cost estimate before any work begins.
How much does a small bathroom remodel cost?+
A small bathroom remodel is priced by finishes and whether the layout changes. Small bathrooms are one of our most popular projects — we maximize storage, light and the feel of space.
Do you install walk-in showers and do shower remodels?+
Yes. Walk-in shower installation and shower remodels are a core service — curbless and low-threshold showers, custom tile, glass enclosures, niches and full waterproofing for homes and businesses across NYC.
Are you bathroom remodelers and contractors near me?+
Yes — Sani Building Corp works as bathroom remodelers and contractors across all five NYC boroughs plus Long Island, so there's a crew near you. Call (332) 277-0990 for a free on-site estimate at your home or business.
