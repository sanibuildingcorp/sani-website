Common Questions

Bathroom Renovation FAQs

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in NYC?
NYC bathroom renovations typically range from $15,000–$25,000 for a mid-range renovation to $40,000–$80,000+ for a luxury renovation. Smaller refreshes start around $8,000. We provide free detailed estimates.
How long does a bathroom renovation take?
A typical full bathroom renovation takes 3–5 weeks from demo to completion. Smaller projects like tile replacement take 1–2 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline before starting any project.
Do you handle all the trades or do I need other contractors?
We handle everything in-house — demolition, waterproofing, tile, plumbing, electrical, carpentry, and painting. You deal with one contractor who manages the entire project from start to finish.
Do you do waterproofing before tiling?
Absolutely — proper waterproofing is non-negotiable. We use industry-standard waterproofing membranes in all wet areas before any tile is installed. This is what separates a proper renovation from one that fails in a few years.
Can you renovate a very small NYC bathroom?
Yes — we specialize in small NYC bathrooms. We're experts at maximizing small spaces with clever storage solutions, large-format tiles that make rooms feel bigger, and custom fixtures sized for compact layouts.
Do you serve Long Island and Nassau County?
Yes — we renovate bathrooms throughout Long Island, Nassau County, and Long Island City in addition to all 5 NYC boroughs. These areas are within our standard service area at no extra charge.
