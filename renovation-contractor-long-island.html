How much does renovation cost in Long Island?
Renovation costs in Long Island vary widely. Single-room renovations from $8,000, full apartment renovations $40,000–$150,000+, whole-home renovations $80,000–$400,000+. We provide free detailed estimates.
How long does a renovation take in Long Island?
Project timelines in Long Island vary: single room 1–3 weeks, full apartment 8–16 weeks, whole home 3–9 months. We provide accurate timelines after consultation.
What services do you offer?
We are a full-service renovation contractor offering bathroom renovation, kitchen renovation, painting, flooring, carpentry, water damage restoration, and complete home renovations.

Yes — we serve all neighborhoods throughout Long Island. Our team regularly works in Garden City, Great Neck, Manhasset, Roslyn, Hempstead, and surrounding areas.
