Common Questions

Carpentry Services FAQs

How much does custom carpentry cost in NYC?
Custom carpentry pricing varies widely by project. Built-in shelving typically starts at $1,500–$3,000, trim installation at $8–$15 per linear foot, and custom cabinetry from $500 per linear foot. We provide free detailed estimates.
Can you match existing millwork in my apartment?
Yes — matching existing profiles, wood species and finishes is one of our specialties. We routinely match historic millwork in Brooklyn brownstones and pre-war Manhattan apartments.
Do you do antique furniture restoration?
Yes. We restore antique furniture including hutches, dining tables, desks, chairs and heirloom pieces. We use period-appropriate techniques and materials to preserve authenticity while restoring function and beauty.
How long does a custom built-in project take?
A typical custom built-in project takes 1–3 weeks from design approval to installation, depending on complexity. We design, build and install in our own shop for quality control.
Do you serve Long Island and Nassau County?
Yes — we regularly work throughout Long Island, Nassau County, and Long Island City in addition to all NYC boroughs. These areas are within our standard service area.
Are you insured for carpentry work in NYC?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured in New York State for all carpentry and renovation work. We carry full liability insurance and workers' compensation on every project.
