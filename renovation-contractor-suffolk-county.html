Common Suffolk County Questions

Renovation Questions from Suffolk County Homeowners

Do you handle town permits for Suffolk County renovations?
Yes. We manage all required town permits across Suffolk's 10 towns — Huntington, Smithtown, Babylon, Islip, Brookhaven, Southold, Southampton, East Hampton, Riverhead, and Shelter Island — including plumbing, electrical, structural, and architectural review.
Do you work in The Hamptons and the East End?
Absolutely. We work throughout the East End — Southampton, East Hampton, Sag Harbor, Bridgehampton, Montauk — handling everything from summer-home refreshes to full luxury renovations with architectural review compliance.
How long does a typical Suffolk County renovation take?
Full bathroom renovations take 3–5 weeks; kitchens 5–8 weeks. Full-home remodels, additions, and Hamptons projects vary based on scope — typically 3–6 months. We provide a detailed timeline at quote.
Do you do additions, dormers, and second stories?
Yes — we handle additions, dormers, second-story builds, and full home expansions throughout Suffolk County, including all required engineering, permits, and town-board approvals.
What Suffolk County towns do you serve?
All of Suffolk County — Huntington, Smithtown, Babylon, Islip, Brookhaven, Southampton, East Hampton, Southold, Riverhead, Shelter Island, plus hamlets like Cold Spring Harbor, Stony Brook, Bay Shore, Sayville, Patchogue, Sag Harbor, and Montauk.
Can I see real Suffolk County renovation projects?
Yes — view our portfolio of completed bathroom and kitchen renovations across NYC and Long Island. We can also arrange in-person walkthroughs of completed Suffolk projects with permission from past clients.
