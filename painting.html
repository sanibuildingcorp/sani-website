Our Process

How We Paint Your Space
Step by Step

01
Color Consultation

We meet with you to discuss colors, finishes, and timeline. Free written estimate with paint and labor breakdown.

02
Surface Preparation

Move and cover furniture, protect floors, remove outlets and switches, and prepare all surfaces for painting.

03
Patching & Repair

Fill all holes, cracks, and dents. Repair any drywall damage and sand smooth for a flawless finish.

04
Skim Coating

For premium results, we apply skim coat to walls for perfectly smooth surface — eliminating all imperfections.

05
Primer Application

Apply quality primer to ensure even color coverage and proper paint adhesion on all surfaces.

06
First Coat

Apply first coat of premium paint using professional rollers and brushes for smooth, streak-free finish.

07
Second Coat & Trim

Apply final coat plus cut in trim, baseboards, doors, and windows with precision and clean lines.

08
Cleanup & Walkthrough

Move furniture back, reinstall outlets, full cleanup, and final walkthrough to ensure your complete satisfaction.

Common Questions

Painting Services FAQs

How much does interior painting cost in NYC?
Interior painting in NYC typically ranges from $2–$5 per square foot depending on the condition of the walls, number of coats, and type of paint. We provide free detailed estimates after assessing your space.
Do you provide the paint or do I need to buy it?
We can supply all materials or work with paint you already have. We use Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams premium paints. All materials are included in our estimate.
How long does painting a typical NYC apartment take?
A standard 2-bedroom apartment typically takes 2–4 days, including wall prep, priming, and two coats of paint. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine.
Do you do skim coating before painting?
Yes — skim coating is one of our specialties. We skim coat walls and ceilings to create a perfectly smooth surface before painting. This is especially important in older NYC apartments with textured or damaged walls.
Can you paint commercial spaces and offices in NYC?
Absolutely. We regularly paint offices, retail spaces, lobbies, and commercial buildings across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens and Long Island. We offer after-hours and weekend scheduling to minimize business disruption.
Are you fully insured for painting in New York?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured in New York State. We carry full liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage on all painting projects.
Do you serve Long Island and Nassau County?
Yes! We serve all of NYC's 5 boroughs plus Long Island, Nassau County, and Long Island City. Travel to these areas is included in our standard service area at no extra charge.
How much do painters cost in NYC?
Painters in NYC typically charge $2–$6 per square foot or $400–$800 per room depending on prep, number of coats and ceiling height. As upfront painting contractors, we give you a free written estimate before any work begins.
Do you do apartment painting and house painting in NYC?
Yes. Apartment painting and house painting are core services — we paint apartments, co-ops, condos and houses across all five boroughs and Long Island, with careful prep and clean protection of your floors and furniture.
Do you paint kitchen cabinets and trim?
Yes. We paint and refinish kitchen cabinets, trim, doors and built-ins for a smooth, factory-like finish that refreshes a room without a full remodel.
What if I'm not happy with the paint job?
We guarantee our work. If you're not 100% satisfied, we come back and fix it at no additional charge. This commitment is why we maintain a 4.9★ Google rating across all our painting projects.
