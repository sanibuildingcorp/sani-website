Our Process

How We Kitchen Manhattan Properties
Step by Step

01
Design Consultation

On-site visit to discuss your vision, take measurements, and provide a detailed written estimate with material recommendations.

02
Demolition

Careful removal of old cabinets, countertops, appliances, and flooring. Protect adjacent areas.

03
Plumbing & Electrical

Update plumbing for sinks and dishwashers. Upgrade electrical for modern appliances and lighting.

04
Flooring & Drywall

Install new flooring (if needed), patch and repair drywall for clean, level surfaces.

05
Cabinet Installation

Professional installation of custom or semi-custom cabinets with precise leveling and hardware.

06
Countertops & Backsplash

Template, cut, and install quartz, granite, or marble countertops. Install tile backsplash.

07
Appliances & Fixtures

Install all appliances (refrigerator, range, dishwasher, microwave). Install sinks, faucets, and lighting.

08
Final Walkthrough

Complete cleanup, painting touch-ups, and final walkthrough with warranty documentation.

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Manhattan?
Kitchen renovations in Manhattan range from $25,000-$40,000 for mid-range to $60,000-$100,000+ for high-end gut renovations. Cabinet refacing starts around $8,000.
How long does a kitchen renovation take in Manhattan?
A typical Manhattan kitchen renovation takes 4-8 weeks from demo to completion. Smaller projects like cabinet replacement take 1-2 weeks.
