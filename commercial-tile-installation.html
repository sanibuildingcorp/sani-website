Commercial Tile Installation Step by Step

01
Site Visit

Free on-site visit. We assess the space, existing substrate, scope of work, and help select the right tile for commercial use.

02
Scheduling

We plan work around your business hours. Night, weekend, or phased installation — whatever causes zero disruption.

03
Material Selection

Commercial-grade porcelain, slip-resistant options, large-format tiles. We recommend the right product for your traffic volume and aesthetic.

04
Site Prep

Protect adjacent areas, remove existing flooring if needed, inspect and repair substrate. A solid base is everything.

05
Waterproofing

For wet areas (restrooms, kitchens), we apply professional waterproof membrane before any tile is set.

06
Tile Installation

Precision layout, large-format trowel technique, back-buttered tiles, flatness checking. Commercial jobs demand zero lippage.

07
Grouting & Epoxy

Standard or epoxy grout per your environment. Epoxy for kitchens and high-traffic areas — virtually stain-proof.

08
Inspection & Handover

Walk-through with your manager. Every joint, every edge, every cut inspected before we leave.

Common Questions

Commercial Tile Installation FAQs

How much does commercial tile installation cost in NYC?
Commercial tile installation in NYC typically runs $18–$45 per square foot installed, depending on tile type, subfloor conditions, and overnight/weekend scheduling requirements. We provide free on-site estimates with detailed breakdowns.
Can you install tile in a restaurant without closing?
Yes. We regularly install tile in NYC restaurants overnight — after last service and done before morning open. We prepare all materials in advance so the actual install is fast and clean.
Do you install slip-resistant tile for commercial kitchens?
Yes. Commercial kitchen floors require slip-resistant, NSF-rated tile. We source and install commercial-grade porcelain with appropriate COF (coefficient of friction) ratings for kitchen environments.
What size tile do you recommend for retail floors?
Large-format porcelain (24x24 or 24x48) is the most popular choice for retail — fewer grout lines, cleaner look, and it photographs well. We've installed large-format tile in national-brand beauty retail stores throughout Manhattan.
Are you insured for commercial work in NYC?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured for commercial tile installation work. We carry liability insurance that meets the requirements of NYC commercial building managers and property management companies.
Do you work in occupied office buildings?
Yes. We coordinate with building management to schedule tile installation during evenings and weekends so office operations are not disrupted. All work areas are protected and cleaned before tenants arrive.
Can you handle a multi-unit building tile project?
Yes. We work with property managers to tile multiple apartments, common areas, laundry rooms, and hallways across entire buildings. We scale our team to match the scope.
Do you serve Manhattan for commercial tile work?
Yes. Manhattan is a primary market for our commercial tile work — restaurants in the Meatpacking District, retail in SoHo, offices in Midtown, and buildings throughout the island. We serve all five NYC boroughs plus Long Island and Nassau County.
