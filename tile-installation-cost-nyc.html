The Numbers

Tile Installation Cost Per Square Foot

Most pages that answer this question are written by people who have never set a tile. They give you a national average, which is useless in a fifth-floor walk-up in Park Slope. These are the ranges we actually quote in New York City.

WorkInstalled priceWhat that covers
Bathroom floor & wall tile $15–$35 / sq ft Layout, setting materials, waterproofing where it is needed, tile setting, grout and clean-up.
Commercial tile installation $18–$45 / sq ft Heavier commercial-rated tile, tighter flatness tolerances, protection of adjacent areas, and night or weekend scheduling.
Regrouting (existing tile) $8–$15 / sq ft Cutting out the old grout, cleaning the joints, new grout, sealing. No demolition.
Grout cleaning & sealing $3–$6 / sq ft Deep clean, color restoration where the grout is sound, penetrating sealer.

The tile itself is not in those numbers. Tile price varies more than anything else on the job — a basic porcelain and a book-matched marble slab are not in the same universe. We can supply at trade pricing or set tile you have already bought, and we will tell you exactly how much to order including waste for your pattern.

What That Means In Practice

Three Real Shapes of Job

Square-foot pricing only becomes useful when you put it against a real room. These are the three we are asked about most.

A small bathroom floor, about 40 sq ft

roughly $600 – $1,400

The floor only. This is the classic Brooklyn apartment bathroom. The bottom of the range is a straightforward 12×24 porcelain on a sound, level floor. The top is a mosaic or a pattern, or a floor that needs leveling first.

A full bathroom — floor, walls and a tiled shower

a much larger number

Because the wall area is usually three to four times the floor area, and a shower has to be waterproofed properly before a tile goes on it. This is the job where a square-foot figure misleads people most, and where we insist on seeing the room before quoting.

A restaurant or retail floor, worked overnight

$18 – $45 / sq ft

Commercial rates carry the overnight scheduling, the protection of everything you are not tiling, and tile rated for the traffic. You close after service and open in the morning on the new floor.

Before You Compare Quotes

What Actually Moves The Price

If two tile quotes are far apart, it is almost always one of these — not one contractor being greedy and the other being generous.

The floor underneath

Pre-war New York floors are rarely flat. Leveling compound, plywood repair or a proper uncoupling membrane are all real materials and real hours, and skipping them is why tile cracks a year later. A quote that ignores the substrate is not cheaper — it is incomplete.

The size and shape of the tile

  • Large format (24×24 and up) — needs back-buttering and flatness checking so no edge sits proud of its neighbour. Slower, and unforgiving of a bad floor.
  • Mosaic and penny round — many more linear feet of grout per square foot, so grouting takes far longer than the area suggests.
  • Herringbone, chevron, and patterns — more cuts, more waste, more layout time before anything is set.

Waterproofing

A shower has to be waterproofed before it is tiled — membrane, sealed corners, sealed penetrations. It is invisible when the job is done, which is exactly why it is the first thing a cheap quote leaves out. We do not leave it out.

The building

Access is a real cost in this city. Material goes up in the elevator or up the stairs; debris comes back down the same way. Co-ops and condos restrict work hours, require a certificate of insurance, and often require protection of the lobby and hallways. None of that applies to a suburban new-build, and all of it applies here.

When the work happens

Nights and weekends cost more than weekdays, and for a restaurant or a store they are usually worth it — a floor replaced overnight costs less than a week of being closed.

The Cheapest Honest Answer

You May Not Need New Tile At All

A good share of the people who call us about retiling a bathroom do not need one. If the tile is sound and it is the grout that has gone dark, cracked or crumbly, regrouting at $8–$15 per square foot gets you a bathroom that looks new for a fraction of a tear-out. If the grout is merely stained, cleaning and sealing at $3–$6 per square foot may be the whole job.

Retiling means demolition, disposal, substrate repair and fresh waterproofing before a single new tile is set. That is why it costs several times more — and why we will tell you when you do not need it. Send photographs and we will say honestly which one you are looking at.

Common Questions

Tile Installation Cost FAQs

How much does tile installation cost per square foot in NYC?
Bathroom floor and wall tile runs $15–$35 per square foot installed. Commercial tile runs $18–$45 per square foot installed, because commercial work means heavier tile, stricter flatness tolerances and out-of-hours scheduling.
How much does bathroom tile installation cost?
$15–$35 per square foot installed. A small Brooklyn bathroom floor of about 40 square feet lands roughly $600–$1,400 for the floor alone. Floor, walls and a tiled shower is a much larger number, because wall area is usually three to four times the floor.
Why is tile more expensive in New York City?
Access, building rules and old substrates. Material and debris move by lift or stairs, co-ops restrict hours and require insurance certificates, and pre-war floors need leveling before anything is set.
Is it cheaper to regrout than to retile?
Much cheaper, when the tile is sound. Cleaning and sealing is $3–$6 per square foot; regrouting is $8–$15. Retiling adds demolition, disposal, substrate repair and waterproofing before a new tile goes down.
Does the price include the tile?
No — the installed ranges are labor, setting materials, waterproofing where needed and grout. Tile is separate and varies enormously. We can supply at trade pricing or install what you have bought.
What makes a tile quote go up?
An out-of-level substrate, large-format tile, patterns like herringbone, mosaic with heavy grout work, proper shower waterproofing, out-of-hours scheduling, and demolition of what is already there.
Fixed price or hourly?
A fixed written price for the scope described, once we have seen the space or clear photographs of it. We do not quote tile by the hour — you should know the number before we start, and so should we.
How much is a kitchen backsplash?
Small in area, high in detail — outlets, edges, inside corners and an eye-level finish. Most backsplashes are quoted as a small fixed-price job rather than by the square foot, because setup and cutting dominate the labor.
