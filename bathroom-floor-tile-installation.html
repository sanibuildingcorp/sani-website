Our Process

How We Install Bathroom Floor Tile
Step by Step

01
Free Estimate

On-site measurement, tile and pattern recommendations, plus detailed written estimate with all costs.

02
Old Tile Removal

Careful removal of existing tile and underlayment. Properly dispose of debris and protect surrounding areas.

03
Subfloor Inspection

Check subfloor for damage, rot, or moisture issues. Repair or replace damaged areas before tiling.

04
Waterproofing

Common Questions

Bathroom Floor Tile FAQs

How much does bathroom floor tile installation cost in NYC?
Bathroom floor tile installation in NYC typically ranges from $15–$35 per square foot installed depending on tile type, complexity of pattern, and subfloor condition. We provide free detailed estimates after assessing your bathroom.
How long does tile installation take?
A typical bathroom floor takes 2–4 days including subfloor prep, waterproofing, tile setting, and grouting. The bathroom is generally usable after 24–48 hours of curing time.
Do you install heated floor systems under tile?
Yes — we install electric radiant heating mats (Schluter DITRA-HEAT and similar) under all tile types. We handle the full installation including the heating element, thermostat wiring, and tile installation above.
Do you waterproof before tiling?
Always. We use Schluter Kerdi, RedGard, or similar waterproofing membranes in all wet areas before any tile is set. Skipping waterproofing causes tile failures and water damage — we never cut corners on this step.
Can you install large-format tile (24x24 or larger)?
Yes — large format tile is one of our specialties. We use back-buttering, large-format trowels, and flatness checking systems to ensure zero lippage on tiles up to 48 inches across.
Do you supply the tile or do I need to buy it?
We can work either way. We can source tile for you at trade pricing, or you can purchase your own tile and we install it. We'll tell you exactly how much tile to order including waste factor for your specific pattern.
Do you serve Long Island and Nassau County?
Yes — we install bathroom floor tile throughout Long Island, Nassau County, and Long Island City in addition to all 5 NYC boroughs at no extra travel charge.
Can you just replace a few broken tiles?
Yes — we can replace individual cracked or damaged tiles if you have matching tile available. We also assess whether the surrounding tiles have a structural issue that caused the damage.
