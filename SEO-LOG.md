## Jul 28 2026 - TRAFFIC AUDIT: THE VISITORS ARE BOTS

**Queried Supabase visit_sessions directly (749 sessions, last 30 days). Decisive finding:**

- **ZERO sessions from New York City.** None.
- **556 of 749 (74%) from three datacenter hubs:** Newark NJ 229, **Ashburn VA 212** (AWS us-east-1, the #1 bot origin on the internet), Washington VA 115. Plus ~70 from Amsterdam / Singapore / Frankfurt / Mumbai / London / Warsaw / Hong Kong.
- **82% viewed exactly one page. 65% lasted under 5 seconds.** Only 79 of 749 reached 3+ pages.

- Non-datacenter US traffic is all **outside the service area**: LA 12, Chicago 10, Dallas 10, Atlanta 9, San Jose 6.
- Only plausibly-real local traffic in 30 days: Facebook 10, Trustpilot 5, Yelp 2, Nextdoor 1.

**Conclusion: there is no conversion problem on the website - there is nothing to convert.** The daily “Visitor on your website” emails were ~3-in-4 machines. This settles the question definitively: the website is not the acquisition channel. GBP (456 customer interactions) is.
