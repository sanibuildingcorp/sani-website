Our Process

How We Handle Your
Stair Building Project

01
Free Consultation

On-site visit to assess your space, take precise measurements, and discuss design preferences, materials, and budget.

02
Design & Plans

Create detailed shop drawings showing exact dimensions, materials, joinery details, and finished appearance for your approval.

03
Material Selection

Choose wood species, stain colors, railing styles, and hardware. We source quality materials from trusted NYC suppliers.

04
Workshop Fabrication

Skilled carpenters build stair stringers, treads, risers, and railings in our shop using precision tools and traditional joinery.

05
Demolition (if needed)

Careful removal of existing stairs while protecting surrounding floors and walls. Proper disposal of debris.

06
Stair Installation

Professional installation with proper anchoring, level treads, secure railings, and code-compliant dimensions.

07
Finishing Work

Sanding, staining, painting, or sealing as specified. Multi-coat application for durable, beautiful finish.

08
Final Walkthrough

Complete cleanup, final inspection, and walkthrough together to ensure everything meets your expectations.

Common Questions

Stair Building
FAQs

How much does stair building cost in NYC and Long Island?
Stair Building costs $5,000 - $25,000+ depending on materials, design complexity, and stair size. Simple updates start lower, custom designs and premium materials cost more. We provide free detailed written estimates.
How long does stair building take?
Most stair building projects take 1-3 weeks depending on scope and complexity. Custom designs and antique restoration can take longer. We provide accurate timelines after consultation.
What areas do you serve for stair work?
We serve Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island, Long Island City, Nassau County, Long Island for all stair services. We do not currently service Manhattan for stair work due to access and material requirements.
Do you work in older homes and brownstones?
Yes - we have extensive experience with older homes, brownstones, and historic properties. We can match existing millwork and preserve original character while modernizing functionality.
Are you insured for stair work?
Yes, we are fully insured with comprehensive liability and worker compensation. We provide certificates of insurance to homeowners and management companies on request.
Do you handle permits if needed?
Yes - for stair work requiring permits (typically major structural changes), we handle all required permits with NYC DOB or your local Long Island building department.
