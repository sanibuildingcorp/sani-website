Two ways we convert

Keep your walls, or new walls?

It depends on the tile you have now. We check it at the free estimate — or send us a photo of your tub wall and we will tell you.

Option 1 · Faster, lower cost

Keep the upper wall tile

We remove the tub and only the bottom rows of wall tile, build and waterproof the new shower floor and lower walls, and continue with a matching tile. Your upper walls stay as they are.

Works when: your wall tile is small, like subway tile or small squares, and we can find an exact or very close match.

Why remove the bottom tile row?

A bathtub has a raised edge, called the flange, that is fastened to the wall and hidden behind the bottom row of tile. The tub cannot come out without reaching it. Removing only that row lets us release the tub cleanly and protects the tiles above it.

Option 2 · Full new look

Replace the tile on all three shower walls

We cut clean lines, remove the tub and the tile on the three shower walls, install new cement board, waterproof the walls to full height and tile them with your new design — we help you choose it, including accent walls and decor tiles — then add the glass.

Needed when: your wall tile is large format, or no matching tile can be found, so a patch would show.

Accent walls and tile combinations, matched to the tile you keep

What you do not see

What is under the tile

Tile is only the finish. A walk-in shower stays dry because of the layers behind and under it — and that is where we take the most care.

  1. Solid subfloor checked and repaired where the tub was.
  2. Shower liner under the floor, turned up the walls.
  3. Sloped mortar bed so every drop runs to the drain.
  4. Cement board on the walls, joints treated.
  5. Liquid waterproofing on walls and floor, sealed at every corner.
  6. Tile, grout and glass — the part you see.
