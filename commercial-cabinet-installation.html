    Commercial cabinet installation FAQ

    Do you work as an install-only cabinet subcontractor?

    Yes. Most of our commercial work is install-only: the GC or owner supplies the cabinets and appliances, and we assemble, hang, level and finish every unit. Countertops can be included or left to others.

    How many kitchens can you install in a building?

    We plan the crew size around the schedule, from a 20-unit building to 650+ kitchens. After reviewing the drawings and unit matrix we give a written production plan: crews, kitchens per week and floor sequence.

    How do you price multifamily cabinet installation?

    Per kitchen type. We price each kitchen type from the drawings (box count, tall units, panel-ready appliances, LED), then multiply by the unit matrix, so the bid is easy to compare and easy to change.

    Do you assemble RTA and flat-pack cabinets on site?

    Yes. We set up an assembly area on site or per floor, assemble RTA and flat-pack boxes in batches, and hang them unit by unit, which keeps hallways clear and damage low.

    Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

    Yes. We are fully insured and provide a COI naming the GC, owner and management company as additional insured on request.

    What happens with punch list and warranty items?

    Every unit is closed with a photo log and a signed checklist. Punch items are tracked unit by unit, and warranty callbacks have one contact person at our office.
