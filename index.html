Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free estimates?
Yes — all estimates are completely free with no obligation. We visit your property, assess the space, and provide a clear written quote within 24 hours.
How quickly can you start a project?
We offer same-day response for urgent repairs. For full renovations, we typically start within 1–2 weeks depending on project scope and material availability.
Are you insured in NYC?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured to perform renovation work throughout New York City. We carry full liability insurance and workers' compensation on every project.
Do you work with property managers?
Absolutely — property managers are one of our primary clients. We offer fast turnaround for unit renovations, common area upgrades, and ongoing building maintenance.
What areas do you serve?
We serve all 5 NYC boroughs: Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, Bronx, and Staten Island — plus Long Island and surrounding areas.
Do you handle permits for renovation work?
Yes. We manage all required permits for plumbing, electrical, and structural work. All renovations meet NYC Department of Buildings requirements.
