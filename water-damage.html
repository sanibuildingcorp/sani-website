Common Questions

Water Damage Restoration FAQs

How fast can you respond to water damage in NYC?
We offer same-day emergency response across all NYC boroughs. Call us immediately — water damage spreads quickly and the sooner we assess it, the less damage you'll have to repair.
How much does water damage restoration cost?
Costs vary widely based on the extent of damage. Minor repairs start around $500–$2,000. Full room restoration typically ranges $3,000–$15,000+. We provide free detailed estimates and documentation for insurance claims.
Do you work with insurance companies?
Yes. We provide detailed written estimates, photo documentation, and itemized scopes of work that meet insurance company requirements. We're experienced working with all major NYC insurance providers to support your claim.
Do you handle mold remediation?
We coordinate with certified mold remediation specialists and handle all post-remediation reconstruction. Once the mold is properly remediated, we rebuild and restore all affected areas to pre-loss condition.
Can you match existing finishes and paint colors?
Yes — matching existing surfaces is one of our specialties. We match existing paint colors, drywall textures, tile patterns and trim profiles so the restored areas are completely seamless with the surrounding space.
Do you serve Long Island and Nassau County?
Yes — our emergency response covers all NYC boroughs plus Long Island, Nassau County and Long Island City. Water damage doesn't wait, and neither do we — same-day response in all service areas.
