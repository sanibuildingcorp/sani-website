Common Questions

Wood, Sheetrock & Floor Restoration FAQs

Can you replace rotted wood from water damage?
Yes — rotted wood replacement is one of our core specialties. We remove all deteriorated framing, studs, joists, sill plates and structural lumber, treat surrounding areas for mold, and rebuild with new pressure-treated or kiln-dried lumber depending on location.
How do I get insurance to pay for water damage repairs?
Document everything before cleanup: photos and video of the damage, the source of the water, and every affected room and item. We help with this — our estimates itemize the damage and repair scope in the format adjusters expect, and we can walk the property with your adjuster. We cannot promise what your policy covers, but thorough documentation is what claims get paid on, and customers across Brooklyn and NYC use our written estimates as the backbone of their claim.
Do you repair and replace water-damaged Sheetrock?
Yes. We demo all wet or bubbled Sheetrock, let the cavity dry completely, then install new drywall, tape, mud, skim coat and paint — matched to the surrounding walls so the repair is completely invisible.
What floor types can you restore after water damage?
We restore hardwood, engineered wood, LVP, tile and subfloor damaged by water. Warped boards are replaced, buckled planks re-laid, and damaged subfloor sections cut out and rebuilt before any new flooring goes down.
Do you restore exterior water-damaged wood?
Yes — we repair and replace water-damaged siding, fascia boards, window frames, porch decking and exterior trim. All repaired surfaces are primed, sealed and painted to protect against future moisture and match the existing exterior.
Can you repair interior wood trim and frames damaged by water?
Yes. Baseboards, door casings, window frames, built-ins and wood molding damaged by moisture are stripped, dried, repaired or replaced and painted to match — interior wood restored to its original appearance.
Can you repair ceiling water damage from a leak above?
Yes. We replace damaged Sheetrock and insulation, re-tape, skim coat, prime and paint — ceiling matched perfectly to the rest of the room. If the ceiling framing is rotted we replace that too before closing up.
How much does water damage restoration cost in NYC?
Costs depend on the extent of damage — from minor Sheetrock repairs to full room restoration with wood replacement and new flooring. We provide free detailed written estimates and work with your insurance adjuster.
Do you work with insurance companies on water damage claims?
Yes. We provide detailed written estimates, itemized scopes, and photo documentation that meet insurance adjuster requirements. We're experienced working with all major NYC insurers and can help support your claim from start to finish.
Do you serve Long Island and Nassau County?
Yes — we serve all NYC boroughs plus Long Island, Nassau County and Long Island City. Free estimates available throughout our service area.
