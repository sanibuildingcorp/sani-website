Our Process

How We Renovate Brooklyn Bathrooms
Step by Step

01
Consultation & Estimate

We visit your Brooklyn home, measure the bathroom, talk through your vision, and hand you a clear written estimate — no obligation.

02
Demolition

We carefully strip out the old tile, vanity, tub and fixtures, protecting your Brooklyn home's floors and hallways from dust and debris.

03
Plumbing & Electrical

Plumbing is updated or relocated for your new shower, toilet and vanity, with GFCI outlets, exhaust ventilation and proper lighting added to code.

04
Waterproofing

A professional waterproof membrane goes on every shower wall, floor and tub surround — essential in Brooklyn's older brownstones and apartments to stop leaks reaching the unit below.

05
Tile Installation

Floors, walls and shower surrounds are tiled with precise layout, clean grout lines and full sealing for a finish that lasts.

06
Fixtures Installation

We set the vanity, faucets, toilet, shower fixtures, mirror and accessories, fitted square and tight.

07
Painting & Trim

Walls and ceiling are painted, trim and baseboards installed, and every final detail finished.

08
Final Walkthrough

We walk the finished bathroom with you, clean up completely, and hand over your new Brooklyn bathroom on schedule.

Common Questions

Bathroom Renovation Brooklyn
FAQs

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Brooklyn?
Bathroom renovations in Brooklyn typically range from $12,000-$20,000 for a mid-range remodel to $30,000-$60,000+ for a high-end gut renovation. Smaller refresh projects start around $7,000. We provide free detailed estimates.
How long does a bathroom renovation take in Brooklyn?
A typical Brooklyn bathroom renovation takes 2-4 weeks. Full gut renovations can take 4-6 weeks. Timeline depends on scope, material delivery, and building requirements.
Do you handle waterproofing properly?
Absolutely — in Brooklyn's older brownstones and walk-ups, waterproofing is everything. We apply a professional waterproof membrane to every shower wall, floor and tub surround before any tile goes down, protecting your home and the units below from leaks and mold.
Do you serve all of Brooklyn?
Yes - we serve all neighborhoods throughout Brooklyn. Our team regularly works in Park Slope, Williamsburg, Brooklyn Heights, DUMBO, Cobble Hill, and surrounding areas.
Are you fully insured in Brooklyn?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured to perform renovation work in Brooklyn with comprehensive liability insurance and worker compensation. We provide certificates on request.
Do you work with Brooklyn co-ops and condos?
Yes. We regularly work in Brooklyn co-ops and condos. We are familiar with alteration agreements, building rules, and insurance requirements. We handle all required paperwork.
How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Brooklyn?
A bathroom remodel in Brooklyn typically runs $12,000-$35,000 for a standard remodel and more for a full gut renovation, depending on size, tile and fixtures. We provide free, itemized estimates.
Do you do small bathroom remodels in Brooklyn?
Yes. Small bathroom remodels are one of our most popular Brooklyn projects - we maximize storage, light and the feel of space in compact brownstone and apartment bathrooms.
Do you install walk-in showers in Brooklyn?
Yes. Walk-in shower installation is a core service - curbless and low-threshold showers, custom tile, glass enclosures and full waterproofing.
