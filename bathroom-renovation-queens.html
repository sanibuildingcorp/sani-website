Our Process

How We Bathroom Queens Properties
Step by Step

01
Consultation & Estimate

03

Bathroom Renovation Queens
FAQs

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Queens?
Bathroom renovations in Queens typically range from $12,000-$20,000 for a mid-range remodel to $30,000-$60,000+ for a high-end gut renovation. Smaller refresh projects start around $7,000. We provide free detailed estimates.
How long does a bathroom renovation take in Queens?
A typical Queens bathroom renovation takes 2-4 weeks. Full gut renovations can take 4-6 weeks. Timeline depends on scope, material delivery, and building requirements.
Do you handle waterproofing properly?
Absolutely. Proper waterproofing is critical. We apply professional waterproof membrane on all shower walls, floors, and around tubs before any tile installation to prevent leaks and mold for decades.
Do you serve all of Queens?
Yes - we serve all neighborhoods throughout Queens. Our team regularly works in Astoria, Long Island City, Jackson Heights, Forest Hills, Sunnyside, and surrounding areas.
Are you fully insured in Queens?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured to perform renovation work in Queens with comprehensive liability insurance and worker compensation. We provide certificates on request.
Do you work with Queens co-ops and condos?
Yes. We regularly work in Queens co-ops and condos. We are familiar with alteration agreements, building rules, and insurance requirements. We handle all required paperwork.
