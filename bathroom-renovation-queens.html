Our Process

How We Remodel Queens Bathrooms
Step by Step

01
Consultation & Estimate

We visit your Queens home, measure the bathroom, discuss your vision, and provide a detailed written estimate at no cost.

02
Demolition

We carefully remove the old tile, vanity, tub and fixtures, protecting your floors and surrounding rooms from dust and debris.

03
Plumbing & Electrical

Plumbing is updated or relocated for your new shower, toilet and vanity, with GFCI outlets, ventilation and proper lighting installed to code.

04
Waterproofing

A professional waterproof membrane is applied to every shower wall, floor and tub surround — essential protection for Queens homes and multi-family buildings.

05
Tile Installation

Floors, walls and shower surrounds are tiled with precise grout work and full sealing for a durable, clean finish.

06
Fixtures Installation

We install the vanity, faucets, toilet, shower fixtures, mirror and accessories with quality craftsmanship.

07
Painting & Trim

Walls and ceiling are painted, trim and baseboards installed, and all final details finished.

08
Final Walkthrough

We walk through every detail with you, complete a full cleanup, and deliver your new Queens bathroom on time.
