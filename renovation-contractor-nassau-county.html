Common Nassau County Questions

Renovation Questions from Nassau County Homeowners

Do you handle town permits for Nassau County renovations?
Yes. We manage all required town and village permits across Nassau County — plumbing, electrical, structural, and building permits — for renovations throughout the county. All work meets local code.
Do you work on luxury homes in Garden City, Manhasset, and Great Neck?
Absolutely. These are some of our primary Long Island markets. We specialize in high-end renovations, kitchen and bathroom remodels with luxury finishes, and full-home transformations that match the neighborhood standard.
How long does a typical Nassau County renovation take?
Full bathroom renovations take 3–5 weeks; kitchens 5–8 weeks. Full-home remodels and major additions vary based on scope — typically 3–6 months. We provide a detailed timeline at quote.
Do you do additions and second-story builds?
Yes — we handle additions, dormers, second-story builds, and full home expansions throughout Nassau County, including all required engineering, permits, and inspections.
What Nassau County towns do you serve?
All of Nassau County — Garden City, Great Neck, Manhasset, Roslyn, Hempstead, Long Beach, Mineola, Port Washington, Hewlett, Lawrence, Plainview, Syosset, and surrounding communities.
Can I see real Nassau County renovation projects?
Yes — view our portfolio of completed bathroom and kitchen renovations across NYC and Long Island. We can also arrange in-person walkthroughs of completed Nassau projects with permission from past clients.
