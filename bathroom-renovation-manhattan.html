Our Process

How We Renovate Manhattan Bathrooms
Step by Step

01
Consultation & Estimate

We visit your Manhattan apartment, measure the bathroom, review your building's rules, and provide a clear written estimate.

02
Demolition

Old tile, vanity, tub and fixtures are removed cleanly, with floors, hallways and elevators protected — important in doorman and co-op buildings.

03
Plumbing & Electrical

Plumbing is updated or relocated for the new layout, with GFCI outlets, ventilation and lighting added to code and within building requirements.

04
Waterproofing

A professional waterproof membrane is applied to every shower wall, floor and tub surround — critical in Manhattan high-rises to protect the apartments below.

05
Tile Installation

Floors, walls and shower surrounds are tiled with precise layout, clean grout and full sealing for a high-end finish.

06
Fixtures Installation

We install the vanity, faucets, toilet, shower fixtures, mirror and accessories with careful, finished craftsmanship.

07
Painting & Trim

Walls and ceiling are painted, trim and baseboards installed, and every detail finished to a premium standard.

08
Final Walkthrough

We walk the completed bathroom with you, clean up fully, and hand over on schedule — coordinated around your building's access windows.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Manhattan?
Bathroom renovations in Manhattan typically range from $12,000-$20,000 for a mid-range remodel to $30,000-$60,000+ for a high-end gut renovation. Smaller refresh projects start around $7,000. We provide free detailed estimates.
How long does a bathroom renovation take in Manhattan?
A typical Manhattan bathroom renovation takes 2-4 weeks. Full gut renovations can take 4-6 weeks. Timeline depends on scope, material delivery, and building requirements.

Absolutely — in Manhattan high-rises and pre-war co-ops, waterproofing protects every apartment below yours. We apply a professional waterproof membrane to all shower walls, floors and tub surrounds before tiling, preventing leaks and mold and meeting building requirements.

Yes - we serve all neighborhoods throughout Manhattan. Our team regularly works in Upper East Side, Upper West Side, Tribeca, SoHo, Chelsea, and surrounding areas.
Are you fully insured in Manhattan?
