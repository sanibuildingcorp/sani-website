Common Questions

Kitchen Renovation FAQs

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in NYC?
NYC kitchen renovations range from $25,000–$40,000 for a mid-range renovation to $60,000–$100,000+ for a high-end full gut renovation. Cabinet refacing starts at around $8,000. We provide free detailed estimates.
How long does a kitchen renovation take in NYC?
A typical NYC kitchen renovation takes 4–8 weeks from demo to completion. Smaller projects like cabinet replacement take 1–2 weeks. Timeline depends on scope, material delivery, and permit requirements.
Do you handle permits for kitchen renovations?
Yes. We manage all required permits through the NYC Department of Buildings for plumbing, electrical and structural work. Our team is experienced with NYC building code requirements for kitchen renovations.
Can you renovate small NYC galley kitchens?
Absolutely — we specialize in maximizing small NYC kitchen spaces. We're experts at creative cabinet layouts, space-saving solutions and design tricks that make compact kitchens feel much larger.
Do you work with co-op and condo buildings?
Yes. We regularly work in Manhattan and Brooklyn co-ops and condos. We're familiar with alteration agreements, building rules, and noise restrictions. We handle all required paperwork and insurance certificates.
Do you serve Long Island and Nassau County?
Yes — we regularly renovate kitchens throughout Long Island, Nassau County, and Long Island City in addition to all 5 NYC boroughs. These areas are within our standard service area.
