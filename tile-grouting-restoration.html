Restaurants & Commercial Kitchens

Kitchen Floor Regrouting for NYC Restaurants

Under the NYC Health Code, floors — and the materials used to repair them — in food prep, storage, warewashing, walk-in and restroom areas must be hard, smooth, durable, non-absorbent and easily cleanable, and kept free of cracks, holes and gaps at floor-and-wall junctions and around plumbing. Open, crumbling or missing grout is exactly the condition that fails that standard. We regrout NYC commercial kitchens so the floor holds up to inspection — working overnight so you never lose a service.

Quarry Tile Kitchen Floors

Quarry tile is the NYC kitchen standard and it holds up for decades — the grout is what fails first. We cut out deteriorated joints and regrout with epoxy grout, which is non-absorbent, chemical-resistant and stands up to daily degreasing and hot washdown.

Subway, Hex & Mosaic Tile

Prep areas, bars, dish stations, walk-ins and restrooms often use subway, hex or mosaic tile with far more grout line per square foot — which means far more places for grease and moisture to get in. We regrout all of it, walls included.

Coving & Floor-to-Wall Junctions

The floor-to-wall junction and the gaps around floor drains and plumbing penetrations are where inspectors look hardest. We seal coved bases, drain surrounds and pipe penetrations so there are no unintended openings left behind.

Overnight, Between Services

We start after last service and hand the kitchen back before morning prep. Fully insured, COI issued to your landlord or property manager before we set foot on site — standard for Manhattan buildings.

Grout Color Sealing

Grout Color Sealing in NYC

Faded, patchy or permanently stained grout doesn’t always need replacing. Grout color sealing restores a uniform color and seals the grout in a single application — at a fraction of the cost and mess of full regrouting.

What Grout Color Sealing Is

A pigmented sealer is bonded into the grout line, giving every joint one consistent color and forming a protective barrier against water, grease and staining. It works on floors, showers, backsplashes and commercial kitchens.

Color Sealing vs. Regrouting

Regrouting removes and replaces the grout — the right call when grout is cracked, crumbling or missing. Grout color sealing is for grout that is structurally sound but discolored. We tell you honestly which one your tile actually needs.

Any Color, Including a Change

We match your existing grout color, or change it entirely — light grout darkened to hide traffic staining is one of the most requested jobs in NYC apartments and restaurants.

How Long It Lasts

A properly applied grout color seal typically holds for several years in a residential bathroom and is re-applied more often in high-traffic commercial floors. Cleaning gets easier immediately — sealed grout resists absorbing spills.

Most grout color sealing jobs are completed in a single visit with no demolition and no dust. We work residential bathrooms and kitchens across Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island, and schedule restaurant and retail floors overnight so you never lose a service day.

Common Questions

Tile Grout Restoration FAQs

How much does grout restoration cost in NYC?
Professional grout cleaning typically costs $3–$6 per square foot. Regrouting runs $8–$15 per square foot depending on grout line size and access. We provide free detailed estimates after assessing your tile.
Can you clean grout without replacing it?
Yes — in many cases professional steam cleaning and chemical treatment restores grout to near-new appearance without any regrouting. We assess first and recommend the right approach.
How long does grout restoration take?
A typical NYC bathroom takes 1 day. Commercial spaces — restaurants, retail floors — may take 1–2 nights depending on square footage.
Do you work overnight for restaurants?
Yes. We regularly schedule grout restoration overnight for restaurants and bars — after last service, before morning open. No business disruption.
What is epoxy grout and should I upgrade to it?
Epoxy grout is a two-component grout that is virtually stain-proof and chemical-resistant. It's ideal for commercial kitchens, restaurant floors, and healthcare facilities where standard grout keeps staining.
Can you match my existing grout color?
Yes. We color-match existing grout for repairs and regrouting. If the existing grout color doesn't suit you, we can change it using professional grout colorant.
Do you seal grout after restoration?
Always. Sealing is the final step that protects your restored grout from future staining and moisture. We use penetrating sealers that last 3–5 years.
Do you serve Manhattan and commercial properties?
Yes. We serve all five NYC boroughs and specialize in commercial tile grout restoration for restaurants, retail stores, offices, and medical clinics throughout Manhattan and NYC.
How much does grout color sealing cost in NYC?
Grout color sealing is priced by square footage and grout condition, and typically costs less than full regrouting because there is no demolition. We measure on site and give a free itemized estimate before any work begins.
Is grout color sealing better than regrouting?
Neither is better — they solve different problems. If your grout is sound but stained or faded, color sealing restores it in one visit. If grout is cracked, crumbling or missing, it has to be removed and replaced. We assess your tile and tell you which one you actually need.
Can you regrout a restaurant kitchen floor overnight?
Yes. Commercial kitchen regrouting is scheduled after last service and handed back before morning prep. Larger kitchens are split across consecutive nights so the line never goes down. We issue a certificate of insurance to your landlord or property manager before starting.
Will regrouting help my kitchen pass a health inspection?
Open, cracked or missing grout is one of the conditions that fails the NYC Health Code requirement that floors and floor repair materials be smooth, non-absorbent, easily cleanable and free of gaps. Regrouting with epoxy grout, plus sealing coved bases and drain and pipe penetrations, corrects that condition. We walk the kitchen with you first and tell you honestly what needs doing.
