Common Questions

Renovation Services FAQs

What services does Sani Building Corp offer in NYC?
We're a full-service renovation contractor offering bathroom renovation, kitchen renovation, painting, flooring, carpentry & millwork, water damage restoration, and commercial handyman services throughout New York City and Long Island.
How much do renovation services cost in NYC?
NYC renovation costs vary: bathroom $12,000-$60,000, kitchen $25,000-$100,000, painting $1,800-$3,500 per apartment, flooring $6-$30/sqft installed. We provide free detailed written estimates.
What areas of NYC do you serve?
We serve all 5 NYC boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, and Staten Island - plus Long Island City, Nassau County, and Long Island. Residential and commercial.
Are you fully insured for renovation work in NYC?
Yes. We're fully insured with comprehensive liability insurance and worker's compensation. We provide certificates of insurance to building management and co-op boards on request.
Do you handle NYC building permits?
Yes. We manage all required NYC Department of Buildings permits for plumbing, electrical, structural, and renovation work. Experienced with co-op/condo requirements.
How quickly can you start a renovation project?
We offer same-day response for urgent repairs and water damage emergencies. Full renovations typically start within 1-2 weeks depending on scope and permits.
Do you offer free estimates?
Yes - all estimates are completely free with no obligation. We visit your property, assess the scope, and provide a clear written estimate within 24 hours.
Can you handle commercial renovation projects?
Absolutely. We handle commercial renovations for offices, retail stores, restaurants, and industrial properties throughout NYC. We work after-hours and weekends to minimize business disruption.
