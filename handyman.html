Common Questions

Handyman Service FAQs

Everything you need to know before booking a handyman in NYC.

How quickly can you send a handyman in Brooklyn?
In most cases we can have a handyman at your door same day — especially if you call before noon. We serve Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens & the Bronx with fast dispatch times.
How much does a handyman cost per hour in NYC?
Most people search for a handyman cost or price per hour, but we don't charge by the hour. We give every customer a free, flat-rate quote before any work begins, so you know the exact cost upfront — no hourly surprises and no hidden fees.
Are your handymen fully insured?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured in New York State. All our handymen are background-checked, experienced, and covered by liability insurance and workers' compensation.
What types of repairs do you handle?
We handle repairs for homes, offices, retail stores, restaurants, commercial and industrial spaces including drywall, doors, windows, plumbing fixtures, tile & grout, carpentry & trim, painting touch-ups, furniture assembly, TV mounting, and general maintenance.
Do you offer handyman services for property managers?
Yes — property managers are one of our primary clients. We offer priority scheduling, turnover repairs between tenants, ongoing maintenance contracts, and monthly invoicing for multi-unit buildings.
Do you offer night-time or after-hours handyman service in Manhattan?
Yes. We offer night-time and after-hours handyman service for Manhattan restaurants, shops and offices across NYC. Our crews work overnight so repairs are finished while your business is closed — you open the next day with everything fixed and zero disruption to customers or staff.
What if I'm not satisfied with the work?
We guarantee our work. If something isn't right, we come back and fix it at no additional charge. Your satisfaction is our only standard — that's why we have a 4.9★ Google rating.
Do you offer same-day handyman service near me in NYC?
Yes. We are a local handyman service near you with same-day availability across all five boroughs — book in the morning and in most cases we are there the same day to handle your repairs.
Do you do drywall repair and patch wall holes?
Yes. Drywall repair is one of our most-requested jobs — we patch holes, cracks and water-damaged drywall, then tape, skim, sand, prime and paint so the wall looks like new.
Do you mount TVs and assemble furniture?
Yes. TV mounting and furniture assembly are popular handyman jobs — we wall-mount TVs with hidden cables and assemble flat-pack furniture, shelving and storage quickly and securely.
Do you bring your own tools and materials?
Yes. Our handymen arrive fully equipped with professional tools. For materials, we can use what you have or source materials for you — we'll discuss this when you book and include it in your quote.
Can you handle both small repairs and bigger projects?
Absolutely. We handle everything from a single hour repair to full-day multi-job visits. For larger renovation projects like bathroom or kitchen renovations, we have dedicated renovation teams available.
