Common Questions

Handyman Service FAQs

Everything you need to know before booking a handyman in NYC.

How quickly can you send a handyman in Brooklyn?
In most cases we can have a handyman at your door same day — especially if you call before noon. We serve Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens & the Bronx with fast dispatch times.
How much does a handyman cost in NYC?
Our rates start at $150/hour for general repairs with a 2-hour minimum. Full day rates start at $750. We always give you a flat-rate quote before starting — no surprise fees.
Are your handymen fully insured?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully fully insured in New York State. All our handymen are background-checked, experienced, and covered by liability insurance and workers' compensation.
What types of repairs do you handle?
We handle most household and commercial repairs including drywall, doors, windows, plumbing fixtures, light electrical, tile & grout, painting touch-ups, furniture assembly, TV mounting, and general maintenance.
Do you offer handyman services for property managers?
Yes — property managers are one of our primary clients. We offer priority scheduling, turnover repairs between tenants, ongoing maintenance contracts, and monthly invoicing for multi-unit buildings.
What if I'm not satisfied with the work?
We guarantee our work. If something isn't right, we come back and fix it at no additional charge. Your satisfaction is our only standard — that's why we have a 4.9★ Google rating.
Do you bring your own tools and materials?
Yes. Our handymen arrive fully equipped with professional tools. For materials, we can use what you have or source materials for you — we'll discuss this when you book and include it in your quote.
Can you handle both small repairs and bigger projects?
Absolutely. We handle everything from a single hour repair to full-day multi-job visits. For larger renovation projects like bathroom or kitchen renovations, we have dedicated renovation teams available.
