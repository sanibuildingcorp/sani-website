Our Story

Craftsmanship You Can
Actually Trust

Sani Building Corp was founded in 2015 on a simple promise: deliver honest, high-quality renovation work at fair prices — and show up when we say we will. What began with precise, dependable repairs grew into a full-service renovation company trusted across New York City.

Today we're the go-to contractor for homeowners, property managers, hotels, restaurants, retail stores and offices throughout all five boroughs. One team handles every trade — bathrooms, kitchens, painting, flooring, carpentry, water damage and everyday repairs — so your project stays seamless from first call to final walkthrough.

Led by founder Zura, our crew brings precision, pride and genuine care to every job, large or small. We treat your space like our own — and we don't consider a job finished until you're completely happy with it.
