Common Questions

Flooring Installation FAQs

How much does flooring installation cost in NYC?
Flooring costs in NYC vary by material: hardwood $8–$15/sq ft installed, tile $10–$20/sq ft, LVP $6–$10/sq ft. We provide free detailed estimates including all materials and labor.
What's the best flooring for NYC apartments?
Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) is extremely popular — it's waterproof, durable, and looks like hardwood. Engineered hardwood is also great for NYC's humidity fluctuations. We'll recommend the best option for your specific space.
How long does flooring installation take?
A typical 2-bedroom apartment (700–900 sq ft) takes 2–4 days including subfloor prep, installation, and finishing. Hardwood refinishing typically takes 3–5 days with drying time.
Do you remove old flooring before installing new?
Yes — we handle complete removal and disposal of existing flooring. We also inspect and repair the subfloor before any new installation to ensure a perfect, level foundation.
Can you match existing hardwood floors?
In most cases, yes. We're experienced at matching wood species, grain patterns, and stain colors for additions or repairs. We can also refinish existing floors to match new sections.
Do you serve Long Island and Nassau County?
Yes! We serve all NYC boroughs plus Long Island, Nassau County, and Long Island City. These areas are within our standard service area with no extra travel charges.
