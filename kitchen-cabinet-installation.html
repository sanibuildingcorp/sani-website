Common Questions

Kitchen Cabinet FAQs

Everything you need to know about kitchen cabinet installation in NYC.

How long does kitchen cabinet installation take?
Most kitchen cabinet installations take 1–3 days depending on the number of cabinets, whether they need assembly, and whether countertops are included. We confirm a timeline with your estimate.
How much does kitchen cabinet installation cost in NYC?
Cost depends on the number of cabinets, whether they need assembly, and add-ons like countertops and hardware. We give you a clear, itemized estimate up front — no surprises. Call (332) 277-0990 for a free estimate.
Can you remove and replace my old kitchen cabinets?
Yes — kitchen cabinet replacement is one of our most-requested services. We remove and haul away your old cabinets, then assemble and install new flat-pack, RTA, custom or pre-built cabinets — level, secure and clean — anywhere in NYC.
Do you reface cabinets or install new ones?
We specialize in installing and replacing cabinets rather than refacing existing boxes. If you want a fresh kitchen, we'll walk you through new cabinet options and the most cost-effective path for your space — flat-pack, RTA, custom or pre-built.
Do you assemble flat-pack, IKEA & RTA cabinets?
Yes. We assemble and install flat-pack, IKEA, RTA and pre-built custom cabinets — doors, drawers, soft-close hinges and slides — all leveled and secured properly.
Do you install countertops too?
Yes. Alongside cabinets we install countertops — quartz, granite, laminate and butcher block — plus backsplashes and hardware for a complete, finished kitchen.
Do you install cabinets for restaurants, offices & retail?
Yes. Beyond homes, we install cabinetry for restaurants, retail stores and offices — break rooms, kitchenettes, storage and service counters — scheduled to keep your business running.
Are your installers fully insured?
Yes. Sani Building Corp is fully insured in New York State. Our installers are background-checked, experienced, and covered by liability insurance and workers' compensation.
What if I'm not satisfied with the work?
We guarantee our work. If something isn't right, we come back and make it right at no additional charge. That's why we have a 4.9★ Google rating.
What areas do you serve?
We serve all 5 NYC boroughs — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx and Staten Island — plus Long Island, Nassau County & Suffolk County.
