Real NYC Projects, Photographed on Real Job Sites

Every photo in this gallery comes from an actual Sani Building Corp project — no stock images, no showroom staging. You’re looking at bathrooms gutted and rebuilt in Brooklyn brownstones, kitchen cabinets installed in Manhattan co-ops, commercial tile floors set in Queens restaurants, decks built on Long Island, and repairs completed the same day they were called in. It’s the most honest answer to the question every client asks: what does your work actually look like?

What to Look For

Look at the details that separate craftsmanship from quick work: grout lines that stay straight through corners, cabinet doors with even reveals, paint edges cut clean against ceilings, tile layouts centered on the room. Those small decisions are made hundreds of times on every project, and they’re why our renovations still look right years later — in busy family homes and high-traffic commercial spaces alike.
