Common Bronx Questions

Renovation Questions from Bronx Homeowners

Do you handle DOB permits for Bronx renovations?
Yes. We manage all required NYC Department of Buildings permits — plumbing, electrical, and structural — for renovations throughout The Bronx. All work meets DOB requirements.
Do you work with Bronx co-op & condo boards?
Absolutely. We're experienced with co-op and condo board approval packages, alteration agreements, and building-specific renovation rules common in Riverdale, Spuyten Duyvil, and other Bronx co-op communities.
How long does a typical Bronx bathroom renovation take?
Most full bathroom renovations take 3–5 weeks from demo to final reveal. Kitchens typically run 5–8 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline at quote.
Do you renovate pre-war Bronx buildings?
Yes — we specialize in pre-war buildings throughout The Bronx, including original tile work, plaster walls, cast iron plumbing, and architectural details that need preservation.
What Bronx neighborhoods do you serve?
All of The Bronx — Riverdale, Fordham, Pelham Bay, Throgs Neck, City Island, Morris Park, Mott Haven, Norwood, Bedford Park, Wakefield, and everywhere in between.
Can I see real Bronx renovation projects?
Yes — view our portfolio of completed bathroom and kitchen renovations across NYC. We can also arrange in-person walkthroughs of completed Bronx projects with permission from past clients.
