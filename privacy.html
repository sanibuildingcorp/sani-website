Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

When you ask us for an estimate, you trust us with your name, your phone number, your address and photos of your home. This page explains, in plain words, what we do with them.

What we collect

  • What you type in our forms: name, phone, email, address, the work you need and your answers to our questions.
  • The photos you upload of the job.
  • Messages and emails you send us, and the estimates, agreements and invoices we send you.

How we use it

  • To price your job, send your estimate or agreement, plan the visit and do the work.
  • To contact you about your job, by phone, text or email.
  • To send your invoice and, after the job, to ask how we did.

We never sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for anyone else's advertising.

Who sees it

  • Our team, to prepare your estimate.
  • The crew member who does your job gets the address, the job details and the photos, without any prices. They get your phone number only when we choose to share it for that visit.
  • The services that run our website, email and secure storage, only to deliver those services to us.

Saved on your phone

If you choose to, our form remembers your contact details on your own device so you don't need to type them again. It asks you first, you can clear them with one tap, and they are removed after 90 days.

Photos and your home

We use your photos only for your job. We don't post photos of your home or your name publicly without asking you first.

How long we keep it

We keep job records, such as estimates, agreements and invoices, for our business and tax records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will remove it unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, or to stop texts and emails, write to contact@sanibuildingcorp.com or call us at (332) 277-0990. We answer 7 days a week.
